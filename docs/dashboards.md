# Dashboards

A dashboard is a page of your own: the devices, scenes and graphs you use
together, in the order you want. Only you see yours; everyone makes their own.

**Dashboards** (in the sidebar, under **More** on a phone), **New dashboard**,
then **Add** what it shows, in sections you can move up and down:

- **Heading**: a title to split the page.
- **Devices**: their cards, in the order you picked them, at the size you gave
  each card.
- **Scenes**: a button per scene that sets it.
- **Weather**: the weather at home.
- **Who's home**: everyone tracked, home or away.
- **History graph**: a device's last 24 hours or 7 days.

Your dashboards show as buttons under the weather on **Home**. **Edit** in a
dashboard's header changes it; **Delete** is in its menu on **Dashboards**.
