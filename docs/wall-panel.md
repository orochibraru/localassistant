# Wall panel

A screen for a tablet on the wall or a Shelly Wall Display: the clock, the
weather, the doors and windows left open, big open and close buttons for each
room's blinds with its temperature, and the heat pumps, valves and switches. It
keeps the screen on while it's showing, and refreshes every 20 seconds.

Open it from **Account**, **This device**, **Open the panel**. Switch on **Open
on the panel** there to have that device start on it instead of Home; the
panel's leave button switches it back.

Sign in on the panel's browser with an account of its own, made for it by an
admin, so removing the tablet is just removing that account. Sessions last as
long as **Admin**, **Access** allows; set it to 30 days for a panel.

On a Shelly Wall Display, open the panel's address in its web browser or
dashboard app, depending on its firmware.
